
# Arogyum Health Knowledge Base

This knowledge base contains general preventive wellness information. It is not a substitute for individualized clinical guidance.

## Healthy diet and vegetarian meal ideas

A healthy dietary pattern emphasizes adequacy, balance, moderation, and diversity. A variety of vegetables, fruits, legumes, nuts, and whole grains can help provide a broad range of nutrients.

For general vegetarian meal ideas, combine a grain or other staple with protein-rich foods such as lentils (dal), chickpeas, beans, peas, nuts, or seeds. Dairy foods can also fit into a vegetarian diet when suitable. Examples of breakfasts include vegetable poha with peanuts, moong-dal chilla, and oats with fruit and nuts. These are general meal ideas, not personalized clinical diet plans.

WHO recommends at least 400 grams of fruits and vegetables per day for people older than 10 years and at least 25 grams of naturally occurring dietary fibre daily for this age group. Individual requirements and suitable food choices vary with age, health conditions, dietary needs, and local food availability.

Source: https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet

## Physical activity recommendations for adults

For adults aged 18–64, WHO recommends 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity each week, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity, or an equivalent combination. Adults should also perform muscle-strengthening activities involving major muscle groups on two or more days each week.

Any physical activity is generally better than none. People who are inactive can begin with small amounts and gradually increase activity. Exercise plans must be adapted for pain, injuries, disability, pregnancy, chronic conditions, and individual limitations. Seek professional guidance when appropriate.

Source: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity

## Sleep duration and sleep health

According to CDC guidance, adults aged 18–60 generally need at least seven hours of sleep per night. Sleep quality and consistency also matter.

People experiencing persistent sleep difficulties should consider discussing them with a qualified healthcare professional. A chatbot cannot diagnose the cause of a sleep problem.


Practical sleep habits that may help include going to bed and waking up at consistent times each day, keeping the bedroom quiet, relaxing, and comfortably cool, and turning off electronic devices at least 30 minutes before bedtime. Avoiding caffeine in the afternoon or evening may also help support better sleep.

Source for sleep habits: https://www.cdc.gov/sleep/about/
